Privacy Policy — YT Scheduler Smart

Last updated: 2 October 2026

YT Scheduler Smart ("the App") is a personal desktop tool for scheduling YouTube video uploads.

Data we access

With your consent via Google OAuth, the App accesses:

  • Google account basic info (name, email address)
  • YouTube channel data, video metadata, and analytics

This data is used solely to run the features you trigger in the App: connecting channels, uploading/scheduling videos, and checking monetization eligibility.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens and API credentials are stored locally on your own device only.
  • The App does not send your data to any third-party server.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.

Data retention

Stored tokens remain on your device until you remove the channel from the App or revoke access via your Google Account permissions.
